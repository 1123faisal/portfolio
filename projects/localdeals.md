## About Local Deals Fundraising

Local Deals is a **digital fundraising platform** that replaces traditional door-to-door fundraising with a social-media-driven, app-based coupon model. Schools, sports teams, clubs, and charitable organisations run fundraising campaigns by selling access to the **Local Deals App** — a mobile coupon book featuring 90+ offers from local merchants. Supporters buy the app (saving money on local deals) while the organisation earns funds. Zero door-to-door selling, zero collecting, 100% trackable.

> *"Everyone likes saving money! Local Deals is a seamless and effective way for local groups to raise funds, while offering money saving coupons directly on their supporters' phones via the Local Deals App."*

**Founded by Jeff and Logan Reece** — Jeff brings decades of marketing experience dating back to 1987, co-founding Peak Advertising Inc. and pioneering coupon magazine fundraising nationally since 1990. Logan (Marketing & Advertising, West Virginia University) extended the family legacy through digital marketing and the UValues campus discount program before the pair spent two years building Local Deals.

## The Core Problem Solved

Traditional fundraising (candy bars, cookie dough, door-to-door sales) is cumbersome, unsafe, and hard to track. Local Deals replaces it with:
- No door-to-door sales
- No physical product collection
- No cost to the organising group
- Fully digital, social-media-driven distribution
- Real-time trackable results for leaders and sellers

---

## How It Works — 4-Step Flow

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Step 1 — We Set Everything Up** | Platform creates a custom team fundraising page and individual seller profiles for every participant. All tech, tracking, and setup handled — no effort from the organisation. |
| **Step 2 — Launch in One 20-Minute Meeting** | A Local Deals rep attends one practice/meeting to guide players through logging in, uploading a photo, and adding 15–20 contacts. Fundraiser goes live immediately. |
| **Step 3 — Players Share Their Page** | Each participant shares their personal fundraising page via text or social media. Supporters can purchase the Local Deals App (90+ local offers) **or** make a direct donation. |
| **Step 4 — Funds Raised, Hands-Off** | The platform sends reminders and tracks results. Funds are raised over the following weeks with zero extra effort from coaches or organisers. |

## Key Features

### 📱 Fundraising Campaign Management
- **Custom branded fundraising pages** — organisation logo, mascot, photos, and messaging
- **Individual seller pages** for every participant to share personally
- **Live trackable progress dashboard** — leaders monitor real-time goals, individual member sales, and overall campaign progress; sellers track their own sales and buyer transactions
- **Real-time sales and earnings management**

### 💰 Two Revenue Streams
| Stream | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Local Deals Sales** | Supporters purchase access to the app containing 90+ money-saving local merchant offers — delivered directly to their phone |
| **Donations** | Direct monetary donations from friends & family (including those outside the local community), with 100% trackability |

### 📲 Local Deals Mobile App (Consumer-Facing)
- **90+ local merchant offers** delivered to supporters' phones
- Easy-to-use **coupon redemption button** — offer marked redeemed after single use (no duplicate redemptions)
- **Participating businesses page** with logos and brand listings
- Yearly renewal prompts — platform builds a recurring supporter database that grows each year

### 🏪 Merchant / Advertiser Portal
Local businesses participate **for free** and receive:
- **3 free offer listings** targeting app users
- **Free business page** with dynamic links — address, phone, website, and social media
- **Precise Google Maps integration** — logo pinned to exact location; tapping links to business homepage
- **Push notifications** — send custom messages directly to app users
- **Trackable analytics** — impressions, coupons added, and coupons redeemed per business
- **Free exposure to thousands** of app users in the local community
- Local Deals window cling for physical business presence

### 📣 Social Sharing & Marketing Engine
- Share fundraiser effortlessly via **text, social media, and email** with one click
- Platform encourages supporters to share with their own networks — multiplying campaign visibility
- Sales are **social media driven** — no traditional outreach required

### 🔄 Yearly Recurring Model
- Supporters automatically prompted to renew each year
- Platform maintains and grows the organisation's supporter database over time
- Year-over-year renewal tracking and fund comparison
