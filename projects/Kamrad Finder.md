## About Kamrad Finder

Kamrad Finder is a **travel companion matching platform** built around a core problem: friends and family backing out of trips at the last moment. Instead of cancelling a planned trip, users subscribe through Kamrad Finder to be matched with like-minded, committed travelers heading to the same destination.

> *"We don't want people to suppress their love for travel. We assist them in their planning by listing popular areas of travel and finding individuals of the same commitment level."* — Kamrad Finder Mission

The product uniquely combines a **destination discovery engine** with a **people-matching algorithm**, turning solo travel disappointments into group adventures.

---

## Core Problem Solved

- Friends/family opt-out at the last minute after a trip is fully planned
- Difficult to find travelers with the same destination interest and commitment level
- Users lose deposits and travel money due to unreliable travel companions

---

## How It Works — 3-Step Flow

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **1. Select a Plan** | Choose Standard or Premium membership based on number of trips |
| **2. Search Destinations** | Browse the curated destination list or search for a specific place |
| **3. Get Matched** | Receive an account notification when a Kamrad match is found; accept the match or wait for another |

## Key Features

### 🌍 Destination Discovery
- **Search & browse** a curated catalogue of popular global destinations
- Detailed destination pages with travel information, highlights, and tips
- Filter/search functionality to find places of interest

**Destinations catalogue includes:**
Alaska · Argentina · Australia · Boston · Brazil · Calgary (Canada) · China · Costa Rica · Denver (Colorado) · Germany · and many more worldwide

### 🤝 Traveler Matching Engine
- Algorithmic matching of users with the same destination, travel dates, and interests
- **Account notifications** when a compatible Kamrad is found
- User controls — choose to connect with a match or wait for a better one
- Privacy protection — personal information is not shared with other Kamrads without consent
