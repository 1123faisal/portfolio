[**Kamrad finder**](http://www.kamradfinder.com/)

**Project Description:**

Kamrad Finder is a travel application built on the MEAN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, Angular, Node.js) with the aim of providing tourists with the best locations and experiences worldwide. This application goes beyond conventional travel recommendations by offering a subscription-based model that allows users to receive notifications about events and discounts at popular tourist destinations.

**Key features of the Kamrad Finder application include:**

1. Location Recommendations: The application offers a vast database of curated locations, highlighting the best tourist spots worldwide. Users can explore and discover new destinations based on their interests and preferences.

2. Personalized Subscriptions: Kamrad Finder provides users with the option to subscribe and receive personalized notifications about events, festivals, and discounts happening in their preferred travel destinations.

3. User Reviews and Ratings: Users can provide reviews and ratings for visited locations, helping fellow travelers make informed decisions and ensuring a community-driven experience.

4. Interactive Maps and Navigation: The application integrates interactive maps and navigation features, allowing users to plan their travel routes and easily navigate to their desired destinations.

5. Trip Planner and Itinerary Management: Kamrad Finder enables users to create personalized trip itineraries, manage their travel plans, and save their favorite locations for future reference.
